Application development and operations teams are adopting AI coding tools at an exponentially increasing rate. But despite the unknown risks of an adolescent market, companies have already opened the garage doors for AI coding agents and handed them the keys to the Ferrari. Clearly we are missing something: the ability to reliably keep AI-driven software releases on track and avoid costly incidents that will slow our delivery progress in the future.
